Phase 1: Snapshot & Reconciliation
Before turning the service on, we must freeze the "Old World" and map it to the "New World."
The Snapshot: Export your current user balances from your legacy database/spreadsheet.
Wallet Mapping: If you don't have user wallets yet, create a "Claim Portal" where users connect their Solana wallet to link it to their legacy email/ID.
Sanity Check: Use a script to verify that total points in the snapshot match the total points expected.
